VIIOccasions

Restaurants are our passion.

Let us immerse you in a world of culinary mastery, where dining becomes a form of artistry. We await the pleasure of welcoming you to an exquisite dinner, where every detail is designed to be unforgettable. Whether it’s a special anniversary or a cherished occasion, celebrate it with a sense of style.

Valentine’sChristmasWedding anniversariesBirthdaysFamily dinners

Looking for a place for a birthday, anniversary or your upcoming special event? Given time to arrange it, we prepare bouquets of flowers, birthday cakes, balloons and decorations. We specialise in turning any occasion into a beautiful memory, ensuring every guest in your party feels special and taken care of.

Dress code — Smart Casual

We have smartly dressed guests celebrating special occasions such as wedding anniversaries. Please respect our dress code.

  • No flip-flops or sleeveless T-shirts
  • No beachwear
  • Shorts and polo are fine if you look smart, with nice shoes, sandals or loafers

At your table

  1. Reading glasses are available.
  2. Phone chargers at your table, fast chargers at the bar, and Wi-Fi for guests.
  3. If the air conditioning is blowing on you, or is too warm or cold, please tell a member of staff.
  4. Freshly laundered shawls are available for ladies if you are cold.
  5. We keep Paracetamol, Nurofen and Gaviscon — ask our staff if you need them.
  6. We use no fake flowers: every flower you see here is fresh.
  7. Please refrain from video calls, speaker calls or watching videos that would disturb other diners.
  8. If anything is incorrect or disappoints you, please tell us immediately — we will act swiftly to make the rest of your evening first class.
